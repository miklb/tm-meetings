<p class="agenda-item__label">Background</p>
<p>During the review of the FY2024 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report, a comment was received by a Government Finance Officers Association (GFOA) reviewer regarding the transfers from the Community Redevelopment Agency fiduciary funds to the General Fund.</p>
<p>&quot;Reimbursements should not be displayed in the financial statements as transfers. The disclosure makes it appear as though reimbursements are reported as transfers.</p>
<p>Reimbursements should be treated as a reduction to the originally booked expense/expenditure and therefore would not be reported as an item in the external financial statements &quot;. [GASB-S34:112b(2)].  The accounting department has updated their treatment of these particular transfers in the external financial statements for FY2025 and is now adjusting the transfers that have been made in FY2026.</p>
<p>Instead of recording services performed by the General Fund (primarily staff expenses) for the CRA as a revenue, the General Fund will record receiving reimbursement as a contra-expense (or negative expense).  The reason for this is to prevent the City&#39;s annual financials from recording the same expense twice:  once within the General Fund and again within the various CRA funds.  This has a neutral impact on both the financial standings/report for both entities.</p>
<p>CRA Director Cedric McCray brief the CRA Board during the July 2026 meeting on this topic.</p>

<details class="agenda-item__details">

<p class="agenda-item__label">Background</p>
<p>Tampa Code Section 14-111, passed in 1989, makes it unlawful for any person to sell, offer for sale, give or have in his possession within the city any blank cartridge pistal or other like device made exclusively for the purpose of firing or discharging any blank cartridge, provided that this section shall not apply to the sale, possession or discharge of any pistol or other like device designed to use .22 caliber crimped blanks and/or .22 caliber tear gas cartridges.</p>

<p>Tampa Code Section 16-21, passed in 2009, requires a facility rental application to indicate if (7) the applicant desires to carry, fire, or discharge any weapon, fireworks, or explosive of any kind. <p>Florida Statute § 790.33 provides for penalties including a civil fine of up to $5,000 against local government officials for enacting or causing to be enforced any local ordinance or administrative rule or regulation related to firearms or ammunition.
