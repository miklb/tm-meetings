<p class="agenda-item__label">Background</p>
<p>Section 163.01, Florida Statutes, the &quot;Florida Interlocal Cooperation Act of 1969,&quot; authorizes local governmental units to execute interlocal agreements for the purpose of making the most efficient use of their powers and providing services and facilities in a manner that best accords with the needs of their local communities.  The Participating Agencies recognize the increasing threat posed by unauthorized or malicious us of Unmanned Aerial Systems (&quot;UAS&quot; or &quot;drones&quot;) within the Tampa Bay Area, and the need for a coordinated regional response to protect public safety, secure critical infrastructure, and ensure the security of public events.</p>
<p>The Participating Agencies desire to enter into this Agreement to establish the terms and conditions for the formation of a regional c-UAS Work Group, the joint funding, procurement, ownership, maintenance, housing, and deployment of shared c-UAS equipment, and the operational framework for a regional c-UAS task force capability.  Pasco Sheriff&#39;s Office will be the procuring agency of the Dedrone system components for the c-UAS group with the cost share per participating agency will be $218,333.37 in annual installments of $21,833.44 over the ten year agreement.</p>
<p>Federal equitable sharing Law Enforcement Trust Funds already received are available to be earmarked to budget for this cost-sharing agreement that will support law enforcement activities through the purchase of law enforcement equipment.  The Interlocal Agreement provides that Tampa Police Department may withdraw upon written notice in the event it determines participation is no longer desired.  It is in the best interest of the City of Tampa to enter into the Agreement for the benefit of the citizens of the Tampa Bay area.</p>
