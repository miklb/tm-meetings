<details class="agenda-item__details">
<summary>Item details</summary>
<section class="agenda-item-financial" aria-label="Financial impact">
<h4 class="agenda-item-section__heading">Financial impact</h4>

This resolution adopts the FY2027 through FY2031 Recommended Capital Improvement Budget and Program as required by Florida Statutes and City Charter. In addition, it adopts the FY2027 portion of the FY2027 through FY2031 Capital Improvement Projects program as part of the FY2027 Annual Budget in the amount of $346,736,959.</p>
