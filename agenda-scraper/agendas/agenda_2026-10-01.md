<p class="agenda-item__label">Background</p>
<p>As set forth in the attached Public Emergency Medical Transportation Letter of Agreement (herein &quot;Letter of Agreement&quot;} between the City of Tampa and the State of Florida, Agency for Health Care Administration, the parties have authorized a supplemental payment program for Medicaid managed care patients who are transported to the hospital by public emergency medical transportation providers.</p>
<p>The State of Florida Legislature has authorized a continuation of the supplemental payment program to include Medicaid Managed Care transports for the purpose of leveraging intergovernmental funds with a 57 percent (57%) federal share and 43 percent (43%) City share that Medicaid Managed Care providers will later reimburse.</p>
<p>By participating in the supplemental payment program for Medicaid managed care patients as outlined in the Letter of Agreement, City of Tampa Fire Rescue can substantially increase its Medicaid managed care patient transport revenue with the City contributing $2,331,763.71 as its share of the State&#39;s total federal match requirement and receive approximately $5,231,688.82 of Managed Care Option (MCO) patient transport revenue for an overall net revenue increase to the City of approximately $2,899,925.11 which is included within the City&#39;s annual budget.</p>

<p>The Florida Legislature created the Voluntary Cleanup Tax Credit (VCTC) to encourage participants to conduct voluntary cleanup of certain drycleaning solvent contaminated sites and brownfield sites in designated brownfield areas. The City currently has five tax credits related to Hanna Avenue, Main Street Park, Cypress Housing Project, Channelside Drive Park, and East Hillsborough Avenue and Shadowlawn Avenue brownfield site rehabilitation projects totaling $209,931.70 that are available for sale.</p>
<p>As the City, being a tax-exempt entity, has no ability to utilize the Tax Credits to offset its tax obligations, the City has engaged Clocktower Tax Credits, LLC. to locate a purchaser for these Tax Credits.  Resolution 2023-798 approved the agreement between the City of Tampa and Clocktower Tax Credits, LLC. to provide brokerage services for the marketing and selling of VCTCs issued by FDEP to the City.  Clocktower Tax Credits, LLC. solicited proposals and has recommended U.S. Bank National Association as having submitted the best proposal.</p>
