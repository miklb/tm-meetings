<p>Resolution approving a Cooperative Services Agreement between the City of Tampa, Florida and United States Department of Agriculture to provide wildlife damage management services by removing feral swine in the amount of Thirty-Five Thousand Dollars ($35,000) a year for four years for a total of One Hundred and Forty Thousand Dollars ($140,000).</p>
<details class="agenda-item__details">
<summary>Item details</summary>
<p class="agenda-item__label">Background</p>
<p>The United States Department of Agriculture (USDA) Animal and Plant Health Inspection Services - Wildlife Services Program (Program) provides for federal cooperation with local jurisdictions to conduct a program of wildlife removal services involving mammal and bird species that are reservoirs for zoonotic diseases, or animal species that are injurious and/or a nuisance to, among other things, agriculture, horticulture, forestry, animal husbandry, wildlife, and human health and safety.</p>
<p>The USDA and the City of Tampa (City) desire to enter into an Agreement wherein the Program will remove feral swine from lands managed by the City Parks &amp; Recreation Department.</p>
<p>It is the desire of the parties to establish the terms and conditions under which the Program will be implemented.</p>
<p>The cost and expense to the City for the Program will be in the amount of Thirty-Five Thousand Dollars ($35,000) annually for four consecutive years for a total of One Hundred and Forty Thousand Dollars ($140,000).</p>
<p>City Council does hereby affirm that it would be beneficial to the residents of the City to enter into the attached Cooperative Services Agreement between the City and the USDA.</p>

<h3 class="agenda-item__head"><a class="agenda-item__anchor" href="#item-16111">13</a> File No. NCA25-16111</h3>
<p>Resolution approving Contract 25-X-01628 Sulphur Springs Pool Restoration Phase 1 between the City of Tampa and On Track Life Solutions, Inc. for the provision of Sulphur Springs Pool Investigation and Stabilization in the estimated amount of $1,833,700 for use by the Parks and Recreation Department.</p>
<details class="agenda-item__details">
<summary>Item details</summary>
<p class="agenda-item__label">Background</p>
<p>The Parks and Recreation Department has emergency need to contract with On Track Life Solutions, Inc. to investigate and stabilize the Sulphur Springs Pool. Sulphur Springs Pool is being undermined by the adjacent seawall failure through extensive soil exfiltration via large spalling delamination into the river. The Sulphur Springs Pool is a valuable City asset that is currently empty and at risk of further damage without prompt attention and currently poses liability exposure as an attractive nuisance. Due to the proximity of the spring well structure, delays in repairing the site could result in full failure of the pool and shoreline, which could pose a hazard to the well system. The well system serves as a source of water and any disruption to its ability to operate could prevent the city from meeting its obligations with other agencies and lead to environmental and resident service impacts.</p>
<p>On Track Life Solutions Inc., is a city-certified Minority Business Enterprise (MBE/BBE) &amp; Small Local Business Enterprise (SLBE).</p>
