<h2 id="5-01-p-m-public-hearings">5:01 P.M. – Public Hearings</h2>
<article class="agenda-item" id="item-15449">
<h3 class="agenda-item__head"><a class="agenda-item__anchor" href="#item-15449">1</a> File No. BG25-14034</h3>
<p>Second public hearing for the Fiscal Year 2026/Program Year 2025 Annual Action Plan; which includes the Community Development Block Grant Program, Emergency Solutions Grants Program, Home Investment Partnerships Program, and Housing Opportunities For Persons With Aids Program.</p>
<details class="agenda-item__details">
<summary>Item details</summary>
<p class="agenda-item__label">Background</p>
<p>The U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) requires cities that receive federal funds to submit an annual report detailing the planned use of funds across their community (the “Action Plan”).</p>
<p>The City’s Citizen Participation Plan for the Programs requires that two (2)  evening public hearings be conducted by Tampa City Council regarding any such plan prior to submission to HUD. The City’s Citizen Participation Plan also requires the City to make available a copy of the Action Plan to the public for review. The Public Comment Period will be held from June 23, 2025, through July 24, 2025.  Information on the Public Hearings, as well as a copy of the document will be made available on the City&#39;s Website throughout the Public Comment Period.  The Action Plan is required to be submitted to HUD by August 15, 2025.</p>
