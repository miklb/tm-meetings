<p>Resolution of the Community Redevelopment Agency of the City of Tampa, Florida, approving the West Riverwalk Multi-Modal Network and Safety Improvements Grant Funding Agreement to construct a new multimodal network along West Tampa in connection with contract 22-C-00001; Tampa Multimodal Network and Safety Improvements Project by and between said Community Redevelopment Agency (“CRA”) and the City of Tampa, Florida (“Grant Recipient”) on property located within the boundaries of the West Tampa Community Redevelopment Area; authorizing execution of the Community Redevelopment Grant Funding Agreement by the Chair of the CRA and attestation by the CRA Secretary.</p>
<details class="agenda-item__details">
<summary>Item details</summary>
<p class="agenda-item__label">Background</p>
<p>The project will complete the West Riverwalk within the West Tampa Community Redevelopment Area and will include, but not be limited to, the construction of a shared-use bicycle and pedestrian path along the western bank of the Hillsborough River from the center of West Cass Street to a point near Willow Avenue. At this northern riverfront end, the pathway will include an east-west connection to North Rome Avenue and will include pedestrian improvements through this corridor. The project will exclude a portion of the West Riverwalk path portion between the eastern right-of-way lines of both North Willow Avenue and North Oregon Avenue which portion is to be constructed by the Rome Yard Developer, Related Group, per their Master Development Agreement.</p>
